About Clevora

Clevora is a small, free tool website for beginners in the USA. It started with one practical problem: remote job posts that look real but are not.

What we make

Our first tool is the Job Scam Checker. Paste a remote job post, or just its link, and it checks the text against 10 common scam warning signs, in plain words. It also checks the link itself. The check runs only in your browser. It is free, it needs no signup, and nothing you paste is uploaded or stored by us.

The checker is based on our beginner guide, How to Spot Remote Job Scams: 10 Red Flags Beginners Must Know, published on Remote Jobs for Beginners.

Who runs Clevora

Clevora is run by Muhammad Sanwal. He builds free, beginner-friendly tools and guides for people starting out with remote work in the USA.

What Clevora is not

Clevora is not a recruitment agency, an employer, or a job board. It does not promise you a job or any income, and it cannot guarantee that any job post is real or fake. It is a warning tool only — always verify a job yourself on the company's own official careers page before you apply, share personal details, or send any money.
