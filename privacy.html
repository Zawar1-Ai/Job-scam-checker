Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026. This policy explains what Clevora (https://www.clevora.website/) collects, and what it does not collect.

What you paste into the Job Scam Checker

The Job Scam Checker runs entirely in your browser, on your device. Nothing you paste is uploaded, stored, shared, or sold by us. When you close the page, the text is gone. Please still never paste your own passwords, bank logins, or bank details into any website, including this one — you never need them for a job check.

What we do not collect

We do not ask you to sign up, and we do not collect your name, email address, or payment details through this website. The only contact route is the email address on our Contact page, which you use only if you choose to write to us.

Hosting and basic logs

Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep basic, short-term server logs — such as the page requested, the date and time, and general browser information — to keep the site running and secure. We do not use those logs to identify you personally, and we do not sell them.

Cookies and advertising

The Job Scam Checker itself does not set tracking cookies. At present, this site shows no advertisements.

If advertising is added in the future, including through Google AdSense, Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. For more information about how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Links to other websites

This site links to other websites, for example the Remote Jobs for Beginners guide and the FTC reporting site. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their content or practices.

Children

This site is intended for adults looking for work. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.
