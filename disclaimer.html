Warning tool only

The Job Scam Checker on Clevora is a warning tool only. It looks for common scam wording in the text you paste and for warning signs in a link. It cannot guarantee that a job is real or fake. A clever scam post may contain none of these phrases, and a real post may contain one by accident. A “Safe” result is not proof a job is real, and a flagged result is not proof it is a scam.

Not legal or financial advice

Nothing on this site is legal, financial, tax, or employment advice. If money has been taken, or if you have shared bank details or a Social Security number, contact your bank right away, save the messages and the job post, report the listing where you found it, and consider reporting it to your local police and, in the USA, at reportfraud.ftc.gov.

Always verify yourself

Always verify a job yourself on the company's own official careers page — type the website address yourself instead of clicking a link you were sent — before you apply, share any personal details, or send any money. If a post asks you to pay to start work, stop: that alone is enough reason to walk away.

No job or income promise

Clevora does not promise you a job, an interview, or any income, and it is not a recruitment agency, an employer, or a job board.
